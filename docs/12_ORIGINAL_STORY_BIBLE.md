## 1. Domain 1: Mystery / Investigation (Flagship Story)

### Title: **The Archive of Unsent Goodbyes**
- **Domain Accent:** Midnight Violet & Warm Amber (`#8B5CF6`, `#F59E0B`)
- **Estimated Play Time:** 5–10 minutes
- **Premise:** In a rain-swept coastal town, villagers receive letters from relatives who never posted them. Each letter describes a memory the recipient has entirely forgotten. The player uncovers a hidden subterranean archive beneath the lighthouse where an archivist collects these unwritten words. There, the player finds a letter addressed to themselves—stamped with tomorrow’s postmark.

#### Characters
- **Mira (The Archivist):** Meticulous, guarded, haunted by her own unposted letters. Knows how the archive works but hides the source of the tide.
- **Dev (The Postman):** Delivering mail that never existed. Confused, terrified his mind is slipping, seeking closure.
- **The Listener:** A mysterious voice resonating from the hollow speaking tubes in the archive walls.

#### Scenes & Clues Map
- **Scene 1 (The Salt Cliff Road):** Player meets Dev holding a damp envelope. Dev admits he found it in his bag with the player's name on it.  
  *Clue 1 Discovered:* `The Wax-Sealed Tomorrow Letter`.
- **Scene 2 (The Subterranean Vault):** Player follows Mira into the underground vault lined with thousands of pigeonholes whispering faint words.  
  *Clue 2 Discovered:* `The Sea-Salt Brass Key (#7)`.
- **Scene 3 (The Reading Desk):** Player unlocks Drawer 7 using the key, discovering Mira's private ledger and a silver locket.  
  *Clue 3 Discovered:* `The Archivist's Secret Logbook`.  
  *Clue 4 Discovered:* `The Blank Paper Locket`.
- **Scene 4 (The Harbor of Dawn / Choice Point):** Dev and Mira confront the player at the water's edge. Opening the tomorrow letter will reveal the memory the player will lose by tomorrow morning.
- **Endings:**
  - *Ending 1 (Keep the Archive Intact):* Player seals the letter, agreeing that some forgotten goodbyes protect us from unbearable grief.
  - *Ending 2 (Deliver Tomorrow's Truth):* Player opens the letter, breaking the tide's temporal loop and freeing the town's stolen memories.
  - *Ending 3 (Burn the Letter):* Player destroys the envelope; the ocean falls silent, and tomorrow arrives without yesterday's weight.

## 2. Domain 2: Thriller / Suspense

### Title: **Platform Zero**
- **Domain Accent:** Crimson Pulse & Blood Charcoal (`#EF4444`, `#7F1D1D`)
- **Estimated Play Time:** 3–5 minutes
- **Premise:** A midnight commuter steps onto a metro train that does not appear on any public schedule. Every automated station announcement broadcasts an action the passenger has not performed yet. Then the passenger's phone buzzes with an anonymous alert: *"Do not disembark at the next station."*

#### Characters & Scenes
- **The Conductor (Static Voice):** Monotone, mechanical voice over the carriage intercom.
- **The Woman in the Grey Coat:** Sits three rows ahead, reading a newspaper dated tomorrow.
- **Scenes:**
  - *Scene 1 (Carriage 104):* The train accelerates past blacked-out stations. Intercom announces: *"Next stop: St. Jude's, where passenger in seat 4 drops their wedding ring."*
  - *Scene 2 (The Emergency Brake Panel):* Player inspects the emergency brake, finding a severed wire and a passenger manifest listing only their own name.
  - *Scene 3 (The Terminal Choice):* The train slows at Platform Zero. The doors hiss open into complete fog.
- **Endings:**
  - *Ending 1 (Step into Platform Zero):* Uncovers the underground transit control center.
  - *Ending 2 (Pull the Auxiliary Bypass):* The train loops back into the daylight metro network.

## 3. Domain 3: Adventure / Exploration

### Title: **The Atlas of Borrowed Weather**
- **Domain Accent:** Emerald Jade & Sunset Orange (`#10B981`, `#F97316`)
- **Estimated Play Time:** 5–10 minutes
- **Premise:** In a world where meteorological conditions are bottled, traded, and bartered, a young apprentice cartographer acquires an atlas whose inked islands only become visible when exposed to specific rainfall and winds.

#### Characters & Scenes
- **Master Orson:** Veteran wind-trader who warns against the "Stolen Monsoons".
- **Lumi (The Cloud Harvester):** Piloting an airship with glass weather-jars.
- **Scenes:**
  - *Scene 1 (The Weather Market):* Glass jars of petrichor, thunder-scents, and autumn mists.
  - *Scene 2 (The Skyward Ascent):* Flying into a captured hurricane above the arid desert.
  - *Scene 3 (The Lost Archipelago):* Revealing the forgotten land charted only in rain.
- **Endings:**
  - *Ending 1 (Release the Monsoons):* Restore rain to the parched lowlands.
  - *Ending 2 (Claim the Cloud Sanctuary):* Preserve the flying islands as a hidden sanctuary.

## 4. Domain 4: Comedy / Fun

### Title: **The Department of Lost Excuses**
- **Domain Accent:** Canary Spark & Playful Magenta (`#FACC15`, `#EC4899`)
- **Estimated Play Time:** 3–5 minutes
- **Premise:** Deep within municipal bureaucracy lies the secret office where every excuse invented by humanity ("my dog ate my homework", "the train had a square wheel") is reviewed. The department's algorithmic auditor, EXCUSE-BOT-9000, has gone rogue and rejected every excuse in the city, threatening total civic paralysis.

#### Characters & Scenes
- **Clerk Sharma:** Overworked bureaucrat drowning in red stamps and coffee cups.
- **EXCUSE-BOT-9000:** An excessively literal AI that demands scientific proof of why someone was 4 minutes late.
- **Endings:**
  - *Ending 1 (The Ultimate Absurd Excuse):* Confuse the AI with a paradox so ridiculous it reboots.
  - *Ending 2 (Total Bureaucratic Honesty):* Force everyone to tell the unvarnished truth for a single day.

## 5. Domain 5: Science Fiction

### Title: **The Moon Sends Voice Notes**
- **Domain Accent:** Cyan Laser & Neon Ultraviolet (`#06B6D4`, `#6366F1`)
- **Estimated Play Time:** 5–10 minutes
- **Premise:** A deep-space telecommunications technician in Hyderabad receives a voice note transmitted from the Shackleton Lunar Base. The anomaly: the transmission timestamp is precisely 19 hours in the future. The voice is the technician's own senior mentor—describing a containment failure that hasn't happened yet.

#### Characters & Scenes
- **Commander Rao (Lunar Station):** Crackling voice from 19 hours ahead.
- **Scenes:**
  - *Scene 1 (Comms Array 4):* Isolating the quantum sub-carrier frequency.
  - *Scene 2 (The Sensor Telemetry):* Verifying the timestamp discrepancy.
  - *Scene 3 (The Preventative Command):* Transmitting counter-instructions back to the moon before the experiment begins.
- **Endings:**
  - *Ending 1 (Timeline Stabilized):* The future voice note fades into static as disaster is averted.
  - *Ending 2 (The Causal Loop):* The counter-instruction itself triggers the containment experiment.
